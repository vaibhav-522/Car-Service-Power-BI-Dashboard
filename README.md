## 📌 Dashboard Pages
1. **Overview:** Total Service Revenue, Total Service Orders, Vehicles Serviced, Average Order Value, Average Customer Rating, revenue by model, service type and area
2. **Customers:** Revenue by customer type, loyalty tier and top customers, plus Revenue per Vehicle
3. **Operations:** Revenue by advisor, orders by service bay and booking channel, Completion Rate, Avg Service Days
4. **Trends:** Revenue and orders over time with Revenue LY, Revenue PM and YoY %

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **₹63.3 lakh** in service revenue from **1,000 orders** across **621 vehicles**.
- Average order value is about **₹6,333**, and the average customer rating is **3.49 / 5**.
- Top models by revenue are **Toyota Vellfire (₹9.7L)**, **Camry (₹9.1L)** and **Innova Crysta (₹8.7L)**.
- **Periodic Service** earns the most at **₹9.0L**, ahead of Body Repair (₹7.0L) and Insurance Inspection (₹6.9L).
- Top areas are **Chikalthana (₹6.2L)**, **Jalna Road (₹5.6L)** and **Osmanpura (₹5.3L)**.
- **Fleet customers** bring the most revenue at 36%, with Business and Individual customers at about 32% each.
- **Hybrid vehicles** generate the most revenue of any fuel type (₹26.9L), and **MPVs** lead by vehicle type (₹25.9L).
- Orders are split evenly across service bays, and **Walk-in** is the largest booking channel with 379 orders.
- Advisor completion rates range from **75% to 85%**, so there is room to improve the lowest performers.
- Quarterly revenue dipped to **₹6.9L in 2026 Q1**, then recovered to ₹9.6L by Q3.
